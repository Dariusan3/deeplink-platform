We tried Bitly. It told us a campaign got clicks — not which clicks were bots, why a link suddenly died, or which post we should reshare.

We tried Linktree. It looks fine on a phone. It's not built for routing, it's not built for analysis, and it's definitely not built for anyone running paid campaigns.

So we built Tappr — for the people who actually depend on links working, knowing why they work, and reacting fast when they don't.
