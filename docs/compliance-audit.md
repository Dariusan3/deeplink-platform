# Compliance Audit — Tappr (deeplink-platform)

> ⚠️ **Indicative guidance, not legal advice.** This is an automated read of the
> codebase, not a lawyer. For anything high-stakes, get a professional.
> Audit run: **2026-07-04**. Skill: `compliance-audit`.

Scope: GDPR / UK GDPR + EU AI Act. The product is an open-signup link-shortening
SaaS ("Tappr", `tappr.me`) — anyone in the EU/UK can sign up, so GDPR is in scope.

---

## Risk snapshot (at time of audit)

**Overall: 🔴 High risk** · 4 critical · 4 medium · 2 low

Headline: the product logs **IP addresses**, stores **Instagram OAuth tokens**,
and sends **user chat messages to an AI (Groq)** — but shipped with **no privacy
policy** (the footer linked to `/privacy` and `/terms`, neither of which existed)
and **no way for a user to delete their account**.

## The four essentials

| # | Check | Status at audit |
|---|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | Privacy policy matches what you collect | ❌ |
| 2 | Real way to delete user data           | ❌ |
| 3 | Users told they're dealing with AI      | ⚠️ |
| 4 | Every third party named                 | ❌ |

## Findings

### 🔴 Critical

**1. No privacy policy existed — footer linked to two dead pages.**
`src/components/landing/Footer.tsx` linked `/terms` and `/privacy`, but no routes
existed (404). Personal data collected: email + full name (`public.users`),
`ip_address INET`, `user_agent`, `country` (`public.link_clicks`). IP is personal
data under GDPR. *Law: GDPR Art. 13.*

**2. AI provider and other processors undisclosed.**
Every message typed into the "AI Brain" chat is sent to **Groq**
(`src/app/api/ai/chat/route.ts:13`), plus analytics, top countries and business
context. Real sub-processors: Groq (AI), Supabase (DB/auth/hosting), Resend
(email), Fanbasis (payments), Meta/Instagram (OAuth), Google (social login).
Named nowhere. *Law: GDPR Art. 13(1)(e) & Art. 28.*

**3. No way for a user to delete their data.**
No account-deletion route, `deleteUser`/`deleteAccount` handler, or settings
option existed. *Law: GDPR Art. 17 (right to erasure).*

**4. Admin panel gated by a client-side PIN with a hardcoded fallback.**
`src/app/admin/layout.tsx:22` read `process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_ADMIN_PIN || "tappr2026"`.
The `NEXT_PUBLIC_` prefix ships the value into the browser bundle, and the
fallback was in source. *Law: GDPR Art. 32.* (Note: the admin **API** routes did
already enforce `is_admin` server-side with a 403, so data was protected — the PIN
was UI theater that leaked a secret.)

### 🟠 Medium

**5. AI disclosure — ahead of the 2 August 2026 deadline.**
Chat is branded "AI Brain" (arguably obvious), but AI-**generated** output (weekly
reports, anomaly insights) carried no "generated by AI" label. *Law: EU AI Act
Art. 50, applies from 2 August 2026.*

**6. IP addresses logged indefinitely, undisclosed, no retention limit.**
`src/app/[slug]/route.ts` writes IP + user-agent to `link_clicks` and
`ab_test_events` on every redirect. No cleanup, no retention policy.
*Law: Art. 5(1)(e), Art. 13.*

**7. International transfers unaddressed.**
Groq, Resend, Meta, Google are US-based and receive EU personal data. No transfer
safeguard (adequacy/SCCs) mentioned. *Law: Art. 44–49.*

**8. Instagram access tokens stored in plaintext.**
`src/app/api/ig/callback/route.ts` saves the long-lived `access_token` into
`ig_integrations` with no encryption at rest. *Law: Art. 32.*

### 🟡 Low

**9. No dedicated data-subject-rights route.** Contact form exists, but no stated
access/export/correction path or privacy contact. *Art. 15–22.*

**10. No cookie notice — but low exposure.** No third-party analytics/ad pixels
found (no GA, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, Vercel Analytics). Only cookies are essential
Supabase auth cookies, which don't need consent.

## Housekeeping note

`.env.local` carries an `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, but the Anthropic SDK is not called
anywhere in `src/` — **Groq** is the real AI processor. `.env*` is gitignored and
no env file is tracked in git. ✅
